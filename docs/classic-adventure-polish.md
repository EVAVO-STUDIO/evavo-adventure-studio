# Classic Adventure Polish Contract

The EVAVO Adventure Studio does not treat “retro” as a post-processing filter. A project earns the classic-production label only when its native art, interaction model, actor timing, scene grammar, puzzle causality and failure policy agree.

## Production target

The flagship examples are authored directly at 320 × 200 with nearest-neighbour presentation, integer scaling, controlled palette use, binary sprite transparency and deliberate native-pixel motion. Backgrounds must read as staged playable paintings: a clear actor lane, consequential props, exits, foreground occlusion and one dominant focal hierarchy.

The final game may use modern storage, deterministic state, validation and accessibility, but it must not look or move like a high-resolution game reduced after the fact.

## Good period craft retained

- Illustrated rooms with foreground, middle-ground and background staging.
- Strong silhouettes and reserved accent colours.
- Family-specific interfaces rather than one generic interaction overlay.
- Authored walk starts, turns, arrivals, dialogue holds and scene transitions.
- Physical evidence, visible environmental state and memorable room revisits.
- Distinct storybook, investigation and verb-panel puzzle languages.

## Hostile design removed

- Required progress cannot depend on mandatory death or restoring a save.
- A required object remains recoverable until its puzzle is complete.
- The player discovers a problem before being expected to use its solution.
- Wrong actions receive brief, authored feedback that confirms intent.
- Puzzles change story, testimony, access, relationships or visible world state.
- The player normally has more than one productive observation, topic or route.
- Time pressure uses authored dramatic time rather than unexplained reflex checks.
- Optional hotspot assistance and a journal may improve access, but neither replaces readable scene composition.

## Native art review

Review every final frame at 1× native size. Zoomed inspection is useful for cleanup but cannot prove gameplay readability.

A gameplay room must demonstrate:

1. The player silhouette separates from the current background value group.
2. The immediate objective has at least one visually supported clue.
3. Consequential props read without animated glow or mandatory hotspot reveal.
4. Exits are compositionally legible without looking like modern navigation buttons.
5. Foreground masks create depth without hiding essential interaction geometry.
6. Palette ramps remain controlled and do not introduce accidental soft gradients.
7. Rain, smoke, fire, water and light animation use sparse authored loops.

## Timing review

Input acknowledgement is immediate. Personality belongs in the resulting pose and animation, not in delayed cursor response.

- Storybook fantasy may hold anticipation and fades longer.
- Gothic investigation may use measured walking and portrait silence.
- Verb-panel comedy should turn, react and recover quickly.
- Repeated traversal and repeated failed actions must never become the price of experimentation.

All timing is expressed on the canonical 60-tick logical clock so replay, save restoration and presentation remain deterministic.
